A verse of encouragement

Adrianne

2023/10/28

Hi there,

I’ve kinda ran out of brain power for this week so I’d like to just share a couple verses that I’m fond of. I find these helpful when I feel like I can’t continue on with what I’m doing, whether it’s just uni work or if it’s things that go on in general life.

Psalm 125 v. 1-2:

  • Those who trust in the Lord are like Mount Zion, which cannot be moved, but abides forever.
    As the mountains surround Jerusalem, so the Lord surrounds His people from this time forth and forever.

Psalm 55 v. 22:

  • Cast your burden on the Lord, and He shall sustain you; He shall never permit the righteous to be moved.

I love this reminder that as a Christian, it is the Lord alone who is our sustainer. He will always give you what you need.

He is Jehovah Jireh. He will provide.
