About

Of all the blogs, you chose this one?

Welcome to our blog! We are two students from Belfast who have lots of thoughts about lots of things that we’d like to share. Here you’ll find us writing weekly (hopefully) about many different topics that we’re interested in. This ranges from politics to programming, pop culture to poetry, and pets to predestination.

Please get in touch via our emails (in the contact section) if you’d like to discuss anything that we write about; we’d love to chat!

About the authors

Adrianne – Hi! I’m a student at Queen’s University Belfast studying Politics, Philosophy and Economics. I’ll be writing about politics, news, pop culture, and whatever other things I deem interesting enough. I love travelling when I can and binging various comfort shows. As a coeliac, I will be sharing a bit about gluten free life, whilst also giving my recommendations on where to find actually good gluten free food.

Caleb – Hey there! I’m a Software Engineering undergrad at Queen’s. From my posts you can be sure to expect rambling, brief moments of clarity, and further rambling. I try to write poetry and fiction sometimes, and the moment I make something that isn’t eye-wateringly twee, it’ll be right here.
