Assignment season strikes again

Adrianne

2024/05/04

Just when the seasonal depression lifts, assignment season swoops in to replace it. When will it end?

In reality, it ends in about 10 days for me. But for now, I’d like to be dramatic about it.

And why is it too that the weather is absolutely amazing when you’re studying but it rains as soon as you finish your last exam? These are the questions that need to be asked. I’m not sure to whom, but I’m pretty angry about it.

Just so close to summer though. To beach nights with camp fires, and ultimate frisbee matches where people don’t need to rush off to revise. Fast forward me to that stage and I’ll be happy again.

That being said, I would like to clarify that I’m not not happy at the minute. I’m coping pretty well considering how I usually am around this time of year. And it is almost over, no matter how it goes. So I take solace in that.

But farewell for now, whilst I go bury my head in my laptop and mindlessly type for a while. See ya when it’s nearly over.
