The Good Week

Caleb

2024/02/03

I’ve written on driving several times before now, and my valiant efforts to learn in the face of fear. Well this Tuesday was my test. A test a long time in the making – I passed my theory over a year and a half ago – and needless to say I was quite on the edge for the whole day leading up to it. Mine was to take place at ten to three, a fairly busy time of day. Terry had deliberately practised routes with me that were more likely to be chosen at that time, and luckily he was right. We did the roads I’d been over many times before, I got a turn in the road as my manoeuvre, and at the end I was told I passed with six minors. Sweet victory.

So maybe on telling you this it would surprise you to hear that this wasn’t the best thing that happened to me this week. And it wasn’t. For a little while previously I’d been spending time with, and going on dates with, the most wonderful girl, Sarah. She lives at the Hub as well, and she’s been mentioned here before, if between the lines. Well I drew on all the courage I could muster (more in some ways than driving took) and asked her to go out with me. And to my continued amazement, confusion and joy she said yes.

Doubtless I’ll be writing more on her and us and all this in future posts. All I know for now is that I’m almost in shock with glee; I’m still wondering, on some level, if I might actually be dreaming. All I know for sure is that I am a lucky, lucky man, and it has been a good week.
