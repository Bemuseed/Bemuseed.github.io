January blues

Adrianne

2024/01/06

Well hello there. 

I’m writing this on the plane home from Austria. Last week, i mighttttt have forgotten to put up a new post because we were travelling for most of that day. I’m sure everyone was devastated to miss it. 

Anyway, i hope everyone had a lovely Christmas and a happy new year!! I feel like Christmas is still going, like the Christmas spirit has been here for longer this year. I love Christmas so I’m pretty happy about that. 
But now that I’m heading home, i think it’s time to go back to reality; take the Christmas tree down and heal from the many bruises that I’ve acquired whilst skiing.

I promise to do a longer post next week, probably about how skiing went. Spoiler alert: I can actually ski, however i think it would only be right to do a round up next week of my most embarrassing/funny moments on the slopes. Notice how i know the slang now, because i totally ski bro.

Well i hope the beginning of January has treated you well and don’t give yourself too hard of a time if you’ve already broken a few New Year’s resolutions. They’re made to be broken anyway.
